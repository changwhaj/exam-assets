To meet the requirements, you would need to add at least two service endpoints to VNET1. One service endpoint would be required for VNET1 to communicate with VNET2 over the Microsoft backbone. Another service endpoint would be required for the virtual machines connected to VNET1 to access storage1, storage2, and Azure AD over the Microsoft backbone. Therefore, the minimum number of service endpoints you should add to VNET1 is 2.
