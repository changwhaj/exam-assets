The error could be due to various reasons such as authentication issues, image not being found, or network restrictions. To troubleshoot, you might need to: - Ensure the container instance has the necessary permissions to access the registry. - Verify the image name and tag are correct. - Check network settings and firewall rules. - Confirm that the container instance can resolve the registry's DNS name.
