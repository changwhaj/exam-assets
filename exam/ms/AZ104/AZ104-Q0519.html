Correct answer Box 1: 10 years The yearly backup point occurs to 1 March and its retention period is 10 years. Box 2: 36 months The monthly backup point occurs on the 1 of every month and its retention period is 36 months. Note: Azure retention policy takes the longest period of retention for each backup. In case of conflict between 2 different policies. Reference: https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/compliance/retention?view=o365-worldwide
