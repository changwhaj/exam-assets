We have two important requirements here: The machines must be in a VMSS or Availability Set AND deployed into the subnet. AGAIN: "What should you do FIRST?" To satisfy all requirements you MUST recreate all VMs in an availability set (possible only during the creation) AND you can choose the same subnet for all VMs during the process. OK, You could move the VM to another subnet (creating a new NIC attached to the subnet and removing the first one) without recreating it but can´t migrate the VM to an Availability set without recreating it. Moving to another is incomplete and you would be required to recreate VMs to satisfy the second requirement. The answer is Letter A because the requirement "What should you do FIRST?".
