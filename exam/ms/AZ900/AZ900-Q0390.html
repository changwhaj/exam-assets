In order from most customer responsibility to least customer responsibility, the cloud service models typically are listed as follows: Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) Platform as a Service (PaaS) Software as a Service (SaaS) In IaaS, customers are responsible for managing and controlling the entire virtualized infrastructure, including applications, runtime, middleware, and OS, while the cloud provider manages the underlying infrastructure. In PaaS, customers are responsible for managing and controlling applications and data, while the cloud provider manages the underlying infrastructure, runtime, middleware, and OS. In SaaS, customers are responsible only for managing and controlling their data and how they use the software, while the cloud provider manages everything else, including the application itself and the underlying infrastructure.
