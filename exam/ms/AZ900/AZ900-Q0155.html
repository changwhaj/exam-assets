Ringi
2020-02-13 18:39
The answer is B, just because of this: MS guarantees that VM's deployed in AZ's in the same regions are in different fault domains (trivial) AND in different update domains (Essential!). VM's deployed in different regions are in different fault domains (also trivial), but might be placed in the same update domain. So a necessary reboot might result in both VM's being unavailable. This will never happen if you place the VM's in 2 AZ's in the same region.
