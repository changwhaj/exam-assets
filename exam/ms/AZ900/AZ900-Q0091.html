The correct answers are: A. enables the rapid provisioning of resources D. shifts capital expenditures (CAPEX) to operating expenditures (OPEX) These are two key benefits of cloud computing. Cloud computing allows for the quick and efficient provisioning of resources, which can be scaled up or down as needed. It also shifts the financial model from capital expenditures (CAPEX), which are typically large, upfront investments in hardware and infrastructure, to operating expenditures (OPEX), which are ongoing costs for services and usage.
