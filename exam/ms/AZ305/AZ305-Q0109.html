B. Azure Policy Azure Policy is the right choice to address these requirements. Azure Policy allows you to create custom policies that define rules and effects for resource management in Azure. In this case, you can create policies that restrict the creation of virtual machines to specific regions and specific sizes. By assigning these policies to your subscription, you can ensure that developers can only provision Azure virtual machines in the allowed regions and with the allowed sizes.
