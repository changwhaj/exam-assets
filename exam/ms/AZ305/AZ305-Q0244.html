Answer is correct. Workspace1: This is the Log Analytics workspace where the logs are ingested. Modifying this resource helps manage costs associated with log ingestion. Change to a commitment pricing tier: Commitment tiers offer discounted rates for log ingestion based on a fixed commitment, which can significantly reduce costs compared to the pay-as-you-go pricing tier, especially when dealing with large volumes of data like 120 GB per day. This change ensures that all log files are ingested while minimizing costs and impact on the Azure Monitor alert.

App1: Why Not: Modifying App1 does not directly affect the cost associated with log ingestion in the Log Analytics workspace. App1Logs: Why Not: App1Logs is already configured to use the Analytics Logs data plan. Changing its settings does not address the overall cost efficiency for log ingestion. Change to the Basic Logs data plan: Why Not: The Basic Logs data plan is generally cheaper but does not support advanced analysis and may not meet the needs for alerting and other features required. Set a daily cap: Why Not: Setting a daily cap limits the amount of data ingested, which could result in missing logs and not meeting the requirement to ensure all log files are ingested
