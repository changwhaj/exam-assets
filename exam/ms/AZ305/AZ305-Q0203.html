From ChatGPT, In Azure, granting a user the "Contributor" role at the root management group level does not automatically give them the "Contributor" role for the child management groups. Role assignments in Azure are not inherited by default across management groups. When you assign a role to a user or service principal at the root management group, it means they have the specified permissions at that level only. Any permissions granted at the root management group level do not cascade down to child management groups. If you want the user to have the "Contributor" role for the child management groups as well, you will need to explicitly assign the "Contributor" role to the user at each child management group.
