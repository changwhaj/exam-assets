The correct answer is C. VPC flow logs is a feature that enables you to capture information about the IP traffic going to and from network interfaces in your VPC. Flow logs data can be published to either Amazon CloudWatch Logs or Amazon S3. You can monitor your VPC flow logs to gain operational visibility about your network dependencies and traffic patterns, detect anomalies and prevent data leakage, or troubleshoot network connectivity and configuration issues. https://aws.amazon.com/vpc/faqs/#:~:text=VPC%20flow%20logs%20is%20a,network%20interfaces%20in%20your%20VPC.
