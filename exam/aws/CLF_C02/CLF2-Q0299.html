Selected Answer: C
A: Elasticity – Refers to automatically scaling resources up or down based on demand, not the speed of initial deployment. B: Cost savings – Refers to reduced capital expenditure and pay-as-you-go pricing, not deployment speed. C: Agility – agility is the AWS Cloud benefit that allows companies to quickly deploy and experiment with resources like compute, storage, and databases in minutes. D: Reliability – Refers to the ability of the system to recover from failures and remain available, not to deployment speed.
