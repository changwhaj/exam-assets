The answer is B. Align. The Align phase of the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF) focuses on aligning your organization's strategic goals with your cloud strategy. By aligning your organization's goals with your cloud strategy, you can ensure that your cloud adoption efforts are focused on delivering business value.
