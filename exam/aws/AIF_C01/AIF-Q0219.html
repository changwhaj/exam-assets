Which statement describes how Amazon Bedrock protects data privacy?

Selected Answer: B
When you use Amazon Bedrock, your data privacy is protected as follows: Inputs (your prompts or documents) and outputs (model-generated content) Are not shared with the third-party model providers (e.g., Anthropic, Cohere, Meta, etc.) Are not used to train or fine-tune the base models unless you explicitly choose to do so This aligns with Amazon Bedrock’s security-first design, where data remains within your AWS account and is handled securely during inference.
