Selected Answer: C
The F1 score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall. It is particularly valuable when dealing with imbalanced datasets, such as customer churn prediction, where the number of customers who churn might be much smaller than those who do not churn.

Selected Answer: C
The F1 score is the most appropriate metric when evaluating a binary classification model like churn prediction, especially when there's an imbalance between the churn and non-churn classes. ✅ Why F1 Score? Combines precision and recall into one score. Useful when false positives and false negatives carry different costs (common in churn scenarios). Helps measure how well the model is predicting both actual churners and non-churners.
