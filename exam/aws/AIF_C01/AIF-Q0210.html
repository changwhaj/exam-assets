A. Batch inference Batch inference processes data in large batches at scheduled intervals (e.g., once per day), rather than in real-time or on-demand. It is well-suited for use cases where immediate prediction results are not necessary, and when handling large volumes of data at once is more efficient.

Batch inference is the best choice when: You process large volumes of data at once Low latency is not required (e.g., once-per-day prediction is acceptable) Inference can be scheduled or triggered at specific intervals In this case, the company receives gigabytes of customer data daily and needs to perform inference once per day, which perfectly matches batch inference.
