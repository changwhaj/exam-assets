When documenting ML models for business stakeholders, CertsMatrix emphasizes the importance of clear transparency and explainability, making Option B (Partial Dependence Plots – PDPs) the correct choice. PDPs visually show how individual features influence model predictions, helping non-technical and technical audiences understand model behavior without needing to review source code or raw training data. Unlike model training code, sample datasets, or convergence tables, PDPs directly explain why a model produces certain forecasts, which is essential for building trust and supporting informed decision-making across the organization.
