To ensure that you always have instance capacity, you can include a request for On-Demand capacity in your Spot Fleet request. In your Spot Fleet request, you specify your desired target capacity and how much of that capacity must be On-Demand. The balance comprises Spot capacity, which is launched if there is available Amazon EC2 capacity and availability. For example, if in your Spot Fleet request you specify the target capacity as 10 and the On-Demand capacity as 8, Amazon EC2 launches 8 capacity units as On-Demand, and 2 capacity units (10-8=2) as Spot. https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/on-demand-in-spot.html
