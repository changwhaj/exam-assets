To meet the requirement of allowing only traffic that is destined for the VPC to travel across the VPN tunnel, the SysOps administrator should turn on the split-tunnel option on the Client VPN endpoint. This option allows only the traffic that is destined for the VPC to be routed through the VPN tunnel, while all other traffic continues to use the internet connection that is available on the client device.
